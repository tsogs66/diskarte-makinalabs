## About / company profile

MakinaLabs builds software for marine engineers. We make it simple, we make it clear. For every marine engineer.

Our programs:

- **Diskarte:** hands-on simulators for ship's auxiliary machinery. Operate real panels, follow real procedures, and find faults the way you would on board.
- **Tank Chief:** tank sounding and bunkering.
- **Voyage Chief:** noon reports and voyage records.
- **Cheng Suite:** the all-in-one suite for the Chief Engineer.

## Upload these

- `chosen/makinalabs-facebook-profile.png`: 1024 x 1024. Facebook crops it to a circle.
- `chosen/makinalabs-facebook-banner.png`: 2048 x 1154. Facebook shows the middle band and puts the profile circle low in the centre, so that area is kept empty.

The `set-*` files are larger master art. Do not upload those: Facebook shrinks and recompresses them harder.

About Diskarte

Diskarte is a set of simulators for engine equipment and auxiliary machinery on cargo ships, made for marine engineers by MakinaLabs.

Each program models one system from its maker's manuals: start and stop procedures, control panels, alarms, faults and troubleshooting. Simulators keep running in the background when you come back to this page.

Works offline. All figures are for training and do not replace your own manuals.

    Design data used
    Starter panels

    Mitsubishi SJ30G: door from Operation Manual 1, Fig. 4-3: ammeter, lamp and START and STOP in one column, breaker handle on the right, thermal relay inside. The box under the ammeter is taken as an hour meter, which the manual drawing shows but does not name. Motor 5.5 kW 9.25 A, HIDENS 7.5 kW 12.7 A at 440 V 60 Hz, Tables 4-2 and 4-3. EM'CY STOP, MM FUNCTION and CONT. SOURCE sit on the automatic control panel, Fig. 5-1.

    Alfa Laval S 826 and P 605: Alfa Laval publishes no starter drawing. The door shows the usual contents: main switch, ammeter, running lamp, start, stop, emergency stop. Treat its layout as typical, not exact.

    Ammeters: square moving-iron meters, scale to rated current then compressed to 3 times rated, red mark at motor rating. Direct-on-line start is shown as about 7 times rating for two seconds. Feed and sludge pump ratings are assumed.

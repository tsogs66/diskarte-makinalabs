    About this model

    Composite marine boiler trainer: shared SI mass/energy plant; four maker faces. HMI labels and sequences are provisional until each maker instruction PDF is mapped — see sim-source/MANUAL-MAPS.md / vault boiler-manuals/MANUAL-MAPS.md.

    Physics

    • Steam from oil fire + EG section into one drum (mass + energy). Oil and EG t/h shown separately.
    • Mimic: one complete-system layout for all four units. Exhaust gas enters the tube bank at the bottom left and leaves at the top left. Furnace, flame and burner are on the right, oil flue out at the top right. Steam goes to service steam and the dump condenser, condensate returns to the hot well, and feed pumps, dosing, sample cooler, salinometer and blowdown follow the model. Burner and control-panel glyphs differ per maker and are provisional. Schematic only, not a maker drawing.
    • Indicated level includes shrink/swell teaching; trips use indicated glass.
    • Fuel grade sets LHV and FO viscosity gate. Status line states EG-only vs oil+EG.
    • Dump Auto PID is separate from pressure→burner; Auto/Manual is bumpless with anti-windup.
    • Distillate air-bound needs casing bleed. SAACKE EG circ has dry-run protection when fitted.
    • Water: recommend → apply → retest. Chloride is never dosed away; blowdown lowers Cl/cond.
    • Cold cascade feed takes sensible heat (no economiser credit), so firing must cover it.
    • Local actions: soot blow, FO filter change-over, and clean flame scanner. Not maker menu trees.
    • Seawater leak drill: blowdown lowers chloride only while it runs, until the leak is isolated.
    • Inspection tests latch: acknowledge → clear the cause → reset lockout. PASS stays off until that path is done.
